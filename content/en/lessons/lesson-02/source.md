## Vocabulary · Personal details

### Упр. 1 — Choose the correct alternatives

1. I'd like to *do* / make a degree one day. — **do** (пример)
2. I don't enjoy *taking* / training exams. — **taking**
3. It would be nice to do / *run* my own company one day. — **run**
4. *Getting* / Passing qualifications is important if you want a good job. — **Getting**

7. When I have time, I'd like to do a *course* / lesson in ancient history. — **course**
8. My sister is passing / *studying* for a degree right now. — **studying**

### Упр. 2 — Decide if the sentences are correct (✓) or incorrect (✗). Then correct the incorrect sentences

1. My sister is doing a course economics. ✗ → My sister is doing a course **in** economics. (пример)
2. I want to study for a qualification in business. ✓
3. I have a degree English and French. ✗ → I have a degree **in** English and French.
4. I hope that I pass my exams this year. ✓
5. I'm training for tour guide with a local company. ✗ → I'm training **as a** tour guide with a local company.
6. I'm taking my final exams in May. ✓
7. My mother runs her own company. She's always very busy! ✓
8. I work part of time in my local supermarket at the weekends. ✗ → I work **part time** in my local supermarket at the weekends.

## Grammar · Present simple and continuous

### Упр. 4 — Complete the conversations with the present simple or continuous form of the verbs in brackets

1. A: What **do** you **do**? (пример) — B: I **'m** (be) a teacher. I work full-time in a local school.
2. A: We like to get up early. We usually **wake** (wake) up at five in the morning and get to work by eight. — B: Me too! I **don't get** (not get) up at five though! That's too early for me!
3. A: My father **has got** (have got) a really big family – five brothers and four sisters! — B: Wow! That's huge! I **'m** (be) an only child.
4. A: James **is taking** (take) an art course this year and he finds it really interesting. — B: That's great. I really **want** (want) to do something like that. Maybe I can join, too!
5. A: I love reading in my spare time. Actually, I **'m reading** (read) a really good novel at the moment. It's called *The Dark*. — B: Oh, I read that last year. I **think** (think) it's awful!
6. A: How often **does** Jane **go** (go) to the gym? — B: Hm, she usually **goes** (go) every evening, except at the weekends. She takes the weekends off!
7. A: How **is** the new course **going** (go), John? — B: It's difficult. I **'m training** (train) to be a nurse and the hours are really long.
8. A: Hurry up, Julie! We **'re leaving** (leave) now! — B: Wait! I **'m coming** (come)!
