## Упр. 4 — Match the verbs in the box with phrases 1–8

Box: do (x3), have (x2), pass, run, study (x2), take (x2), train (x2), work (x3).

1. do / take a course in
2. do / have a degree / qualification in
3. do / take / pass an exam
4. train / work as a tour guide
5. run / have your own company
6. work for a company / myself
7. study for a degree / qualification
8. work / study / train part time / full time

## Vocab explanation 1

- **do a degree in** — здобувати диплом з. To study at university to get a degree in a particular subject. *do a degree in law / medicine.* She is doing a degree in economics.
- **have a degree in** — мати диплом з. To already have a university qualification in a subject. *have a degree in history.* He has a degree in engineering.
- **do a qualification in** — здобувати кваліфікацію з. *do a qualification in marketing.* She is doing a qualification in design.
- **have a qualification in** — мати кваліфікацію з. *have a qualification in teaching.* He has a qualification in IT.
- **take a course in** — проходити курс з. To attend lessons in a particular subject. *take a course in business English.* She is taking a course in management.
- **do a course in** — проходити курс з. To study a particular subject for a short period. *do a course in photography.* He is doing a course in programming.
- Словарь (course): Andy's doing a one-year journalism course. · a course on architecture · I'm taking a course in graphic design.
- **do an exam** — складати іспит. To complete an official test. I have to do three exams this week.
- **take an exam** — складати іспит. To sit and complete an official test. She is taking an important exam tomorrow.
- **pass an exam** — скласти іспит (успішно). To succeed in an exam. He passed his final exam.

## Vocab explanation 2

- **train as a tour guide** — навчатися на гіда-екскурсовода. She is training as a tour guide. (guide — someone whose job is to take tourists to a place and show them around)
- **run your own company** — керувати власною компанією. run – ran – run. He runs his own company.
- **work for a company** — працювати на компанію / у компанії. She works for an international company.
- **work for myself** — працювати на себе. I prefer to work for myself.
- **study for a degree** — навчатися для отримання диплома. She is studying for a degree in law.
- **study for a qualification** — навчатися для отримання кваліфікації. He is studying for a qualification in finance.
- **work part-time** — працювати неповний робочий день. She works part-time at a shop.
- **work full-time** — працювати повний робочий день. He works full-time in an office.

Рамки:
- I have a degree in… — У мене освіта (диплом) у галузі… *I have a degree in Linguistics.*
- My degree is in… — Моя освіта / мій диплом у галузі… *My degree is in Computer Science.*
- I studied… at university. — Я вивчав(-ла)… в університеті. *I studied Linguistics at university.*

## Vocab explanation 3 — предмет → профессия

- **architecture** — архітектура. *study architecture.* She wants to study architecture at university. · **architect** — архітектор. *work as an architect.* He works as an architect in Kyiv.
- **economics** — економіка. *study economics.* She is studying economics at university. · **economist** — економіст. *work as an economist.* He works as an economist for a bank.
- **engineering** — інженерія. *study engineering.* She is doing a degree in engineering. · **engineer** — інженер. *work as an engineer.* He works as an engineer in a large company.
- **journalism** — журналістика. *study journalism.* She is studying journalism at university. · **journalist** — журналіст. *work as a journalist.* He works as a journalist for a national newspaper.
- **law** — право. *study law.* She is studying law at university. · **lawyer** — юрист, адвокат. *work as a lawyer.* He works as a lawyer in a big firm.
- **medicine** — медицина. *study medicine.* She is studying medicine to become a doctor.
- **politics** — політика. *be involved in politics.* He has always been interested in politics. · **politician** — політик. *become a politician.* She wants to become a politician in the future.
- **science** — наука. *study science.* He teaches science at school. · **scientist** — науковець, вчений. *work as a scientist.* She works as a scientist in a laboratory.
